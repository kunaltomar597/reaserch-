# Retrieval plan and verification record (paper 2)

Verification route: as for paper 1, api.crossref.org / api.openalex.org / api.semanticscholar.org and publisher sites are blocked by the environment's egress policy, so doi2bib.py and citations_lint --resolve could not run. Each of the 51 entries was verified with at least one targeted WebSearch against publisher/index pages (journals.aom.org, onlinelibrary.wiley.com, journals.sagepub.com, sciencedirect, link.springer.com, nature.com, psycnet, PubMed, Cambridge Core, university repositories). Fields not confirmed were omitted; where a DOI was not confirmed, a verified URL is given instead.

## Notes and decisions
- Morrison 1993 finding confirmed: technical information sought mainly by inquiry; other types (incl. normative) mainly by observation; normative and social information mostly from peers.
- Joshi et al. 2010 "cohort-based" identity (shared entry into an organization/experience) vs "age-based" confirmed via secondary descriptions of the article.
- Stinchcombe 1965 page range differs across sources (142-193 vs 153-193); pages omitted.
- Mayer et al. 2009, Weiss 1977, Louis 1980, Kish-Gephart et al. 2010, Trevino et al. 1998, Ashforth & Saks 1996, Ashforth & Anand 2003, Gajendran & Harrison 2007, Costanza et al. 2012, Joshi et al. 2010, Costanza & Finkelstein 2015, Campbell et al. 2015: DOI not confirmed by search, verified URL used instead.
- Weiss 1978: only title/venue/volume/pages confirmed; cited only for the (title-level) claim that social learning extends to work values.
- Rejected: Emanuel, Harrington & Pallais (NBER working paper on proximity and feedback) - not peer reviewed; practitioner articles on Gen Z remote onboarding - not peer reviewed; Ravid et al. JOB meta-analysis - conflicting year/volume (as in paper 1).
